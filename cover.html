// Core Capabilities

10 AI-Powered Features

A complete end-to-end system transforming how citizens report civic issues and how authorities respond.

01 — AI Engine
🤖 AI Image Recognition
YOLOv8/MobileNetV3 auto-detects potholes, garbage, broken lights from a single photo — 96% accuracy.
02 — Routing
🏢 Smart Authority Routing
Complaints auto-routed to PWD, Municipal Corp, Electricity Board, Water Dept, or Sanitation instantly.
03 — Intelligence
🚨 Auto Priority Detection
AI assigns urgency based on issue severity, image analysis, and location criticality (hospitals/schools).
04 — Visualization
📍 Heatmap Dashboard
Interactive Leaflet.js heatmap shows complaint hotspots, clusters for resource allocation.
05 — Dedup
🔍 Duplicate Detection
Haversine geo-clustering merges reports within 100m — prevents spam, shows true severity.
06 — Gamification
🎖️ Citizen Rewards
5-tier badge system: Newcomer → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum Hero, with leaderboard.
07 — Tracking
📊 Real-Time Tracking
Parcel-style tracking: Submitted → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved, with full timeline history.
08 — Voice + Chat
🎙️ Voice & AI Chatbot
NagarBot answers queries in your chosen language. Voice-to-text fills descriptions automatically.
09 — Offline
📶 Offline PWA
Service Worker caches the app — offline reports saved locally and auto-synced on reconnect.
10 — Analytics
📈 Gov Analytics Dashboard
Policy insights: dept performance, worst zones, response times, trend analysis via Chart.js.
